Study title

Study of Attention and Task Experience

What is the study about?

You are invited to participate in a research study examining attention and people's experiences while performing a simple computer- or smartphone-based task.

What will I be asked to do?

You will first complete a fractal coloring task. You may continue the task for as long as you wish and may stop whenever you choose.

After completing the task, you will answer a short questionnaire about your experience during the task and about some of your general experiences and everyday habits.

You will also be asked to provide:

  • Age
  • Gender
  • Year of study
  • Batch
  • Information about your screen time/digital device use

The study will take approximately 10–15 minutes.

Voluntary participation

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to participate or stop participating at any time without giving a reason or facing any disadvantage.

Confidentiality

The information you provide will be kept confidential and used for research purposes. Your responses may be analyzed and reported in scientific publications or presentations. You will not be personally identified in any publication or presentation.

How to find your screen time

iPhone

  1. Open Settings > Screen Time.
  2. Tap See All App & Website Activity (on newer iOS versions, tap the daily average chart).
  3. Choose Week at the top.
  4. Tap the back arrow, or swipe right on the chart, to move to last week. Note the Daily Average shown at the top.
  5. Go back one more week for the week before.
  6. For a single app: while viewing last week, scroll to Most Used and tap the app. Enter that app's daily average, not its weekly total.

Android

  1. Open Settings > Digital Wellbeing & parental controls. On Samsung it's Settings > Digital Wellbeing and parental controls. On other brands, search Settings for "screen time" or "digital wellbeing".
  2. Open the weekly view: on Pixel and most phones, tap the circle chart; on Samsung, tap the weekly report icon at the top right.
  3. Move back to last week and note the daily average. Then move back once more for the week before.
  4. For a single app: tap the app in the list. If it only shows single days, add up last week's 7 days and divide by 7.
